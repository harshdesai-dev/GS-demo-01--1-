Facilities & Services

Every Zone Built for Purposeful Training.

Explore the training bays and illustrative service offerings showcased in this Forge Fitness Club studio demo.

Illustrative Demo Offerings: Zone descriptions and service outlines below represent sample studio capabilities for an independent gym website. Specific equipment inventories and availability are confirmed directly with the studio.
01. Strength Training
01. Primary Floor Zone

Strength Training & Free Weights

The core of Forge Fitness Club is an open barbell and dumbbell floor built for foundational progressive overload. Every rack sits on high-density rubber flooring with clear loading space.

  • Power racks equipped with safety spotter arms and pull-up stations
  • Dedicated deadlift platforms with bumper and calibrated weight plates
  • Full dumbbell runs and adjustable flat-to-incline utility benches
02. Functional Training
02. Conditioning Lane

Functional Training & Turf Zone

Designed for multi-planar movement, core stability, and athletic conditioning. The turf lane lets members combine sled drives, loaded carries, and bodyweight work without encroaching on barbell stations.

  • Marked sprint and prowler sled push/pull turf track
  • Cast-iron kettlebells, wall balls, and heavy sandbag implements
  • Plyometric boxes and overhead suspension anchor points
03. Cardio Area
03. Endurance & Intervals

Cardio Conditioning Area

Positioned along tall exterior windows, the cardio bay focuses on self-paced performance tools suited to both steady Zone 2 endurance work and sharp anaerobic intervals.

  • Rowing ergometers for full-body low-impact aerobic conditioning
  • Air-resistance bikes built for controlled interval blocks
  • Non-motorized curved sprint treadmills for natural running mechanics
04. Personal Training
04. Illustrative Service Offering

One-on-One Personal Coaching

As an illustrative service highlight, personal coaching sessions pair structured strength programming with live movement feedback tailored to individual schedules and starting fitness levels.

  • Foundational technique onboarding for squats, hinges, and presses
  • Goal-oriented training blocks aligned with work and recovery rhythms
  • Enquiries and scheduling arranged directly via phone or WhatsApp
05. Small-Group Conditioning
05. Illustrative Service Offering

Small-Group Conditioning Sessions

Represented here as an illustrative studio format, small-group conditioning brings compact circuits of kettlebells, medicine balls, and bodyweight drills into a focused shared room without oversized class crowds.

  • Station-based conditioning circuits scaled for mixed experience levels
  • Emphasis on safe pacing, clear coaching cues, and steady progression
  • Direct enquiries handled via studio phone or WhatsApp
